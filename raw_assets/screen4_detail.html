Project Overview

Hyperion OS is a robust data visualization dashboard engineered for high-frequency trading environments. The objective was to strip away all visual noise, relying entirely on stark contrasts, heavy borders, and deliberate typography to guide the user's focus during high-stress scenarios.

By embracing a Neobrutalist design philosophy, we rejected the softening effects of gradients and drop shadows, opting instead for a UI that feels almost tactile—like industrial machinery. Every interaction is immediate, reinforced by hard geometric shadows that compress upon interaction, providing undeniable feedback.

The color palette is intentionally restricted. Royal Blue serves as the sole indicator of actionable elements, ensuring that vital operations are immediately identifiable against the otherwise monochromatic, highly structured grid.
